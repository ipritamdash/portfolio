# Indian Art Music Source Separation

**Role:** ML Engineer | **Stack:** PyTorch, Band-Split RoFormer

---

An applied deep learning project to separate instruments (Mridangam, Violin) from mixed Indian Art Music tracks.

- **Model:** Fine-tuned **Band-Split RoFormer** on a custom dataset.
- **Challenge:** Western source separation models fail on Indian instruments due to spectral overlap and non-standard tuning.
- **Outcome:** Achieved clean separation with minimal artifacts, preserving the distinct timbre of Indian instruments.
